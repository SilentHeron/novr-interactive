# 0.4.8.1

- The hand option wheel is about 40% smaller and lies parallel to the controlling thumbstick’s surface, following the controller’s orientation.
- Stick-limit feedback is sustained and grows smoothly toward full travel, replacing the slow intermittent pulses.
- Binding pages use Steam Frame artwork and the supplied Oculus diagram, with clickable side labels arranged to avoid crossing lines. Live rings and callout edges show touch in amber and presses in green, where supported by the controller.
- The look cue sits slightly lower by default, with a vertical-position slider relative to the centre of vision in View and Settings. Target selection is unchanged.
- Returning to the VR cockpit restores your selected shadow distance instead of the base game's hard-coded range.
- Free hands follow the controller's pointing direction for straight wrists. Shoulders follow head lean more naturally, and wrist rotation causes less elbow swivel. Fitted flight-control grip poses are unchanged.
- Sleeves have fitted cuffs, closed shoulder ends and smoother skinning across their seams. Copied cloth textures retain more fold detail, with filtered fabric colour and normal detail prepared in the background.
- The stick rotates around a point 4 cm farther down the shaft, retaining its neutral grip position and hand fit.
- Aircraft without a saved HUD layout start with widgets in front of the tracked seated position, within reach. Saved layouts remain fixed when spawning, recentering, or opening Edit.
- Every aircraft gets all nine editable widgets, including modded aircraft with delayed metadata. Missing readouts do not prevent editing, and profiles cannot save under an unnamed shared aircraft identity.
