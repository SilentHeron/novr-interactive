# 0.4.7.12

- One **View → Edit layout** button unlocks widgets, throttle and stick together. Reopen View to save or cancel.
- Seven widgets start within reach: weapons, countermeasures, capacitor, missile warnings, radar/EW, notifications and map. Empty readouts have quiet idle states.
- Grip to translate; grab opposite corners with both hands to resize. Rotation stays fixed. Labels, outlines and corner handles appear only during editing, with a brighter touch/grab highlight.
- Brings obsolete stock layouts closer while retaining customized settings. **Bring within reach** recovers distant items during editing.
- The small map retains native terrain, icons and clipping, with terrain blending corrected for cockpit scenery. Opening the full map restores its native layout and materials.
- Guards against destroyed camera references during mission changes.

Headset testing is still needed for reach, readability and terrain appearance.
