# 0.4.8.20

- Adds optional wrist attachments for every HUD panel. In Edit layout, use the panel's pencil to choose Cockpit, Left wrist or Right wrist. Wrist panels fade in when viewed and hide when looking away or opening that hand's quick wheel. Cockpit and wrist placements are saved separately within each aircraft slot.
- Arranges unsaved layouts on three inward-facing planes below eye level: defensive information on the left, immediate instructions and controls in the center, and navigation and notifications on the right. Saved layouts retain their positions.
- Enlarges the auxiliary controls panel and fits its complete native diagram without the empty space around its original HUD anchor. Animated fan, nozzle and wing gauges keep a stable size as their angles change.
- Reduces near clipping in the tracked cockpit camera for close side windows, including the Ibis. The optional seat-clearance feature now explicitly rejects glass, transparent materials and ambiguous mixed-material meshes.

Wrist panels remain visible while editing. Grip to move or tilt them and use both corners to resize; release returns them to following the wrist. Save the aircraft slot to keep changes.
