# 0.4.8.21

- Replaces wrist attachment buttons with a grab gesture. In Edit layout, hold a panel over the opposite wrist until its preview turns white and says **Release to attach**, then release your grip. A short haptic tick confirms readiness. Moving away cancels the preview; pulling an attached panel clear and releasing places it in the cockpit. Two-hand resizing keeps its current attachment.
- Corrects the collective elbow guide so the upright glove cannot pull the elbow into a reversed bend. Glove fit and collective controls are unchanged.
- Enables head clearance by default at 25 cm and expands its slider to 5–40 cm. Existing saved preferences are preserved; glass and ambiguous mixed meshes remain excluded.

Save the aircraft layout slot to retain widget changes.
