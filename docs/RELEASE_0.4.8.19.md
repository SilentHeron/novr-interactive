# 0.4.8.19

- Adds a faint, brief haptic response to quick, small flight-stick corrections. Slow movement, tracking tremor and holding a turn stay silent; the existing travel-limit cue takes priority. Uses the existing Haptic strength setting.
- Reverses wing-sweep adjustment: forward extends the wings, backward sweeps them aft. Fan, nozzle and tiltwing directions are unchanged.
- Moves the complete native nozzle, wing-sweep or fan gauge—including its diagram and animations—into the **Auxiliary controls** HUD frame. Move, tilt, resize or hide it through **View → Edit layout**. The frame only shows the gauges fitted to the current aircraft.

Existing saved widget positions are retained. The new frame starts within reach of the seated pilot and saves independently in each aircraft's layout slots.

Includes the notification, text-size, sleeve and damage-overlay fixes from 0.4.8.18.
