# 0.4.8.0

- Free-hand thumbstick click opens the native option wheel above that hand. Deflect to highlight, center to select, or click to cancel. Trigger changes Options / Weapons pages.
- Added cockpit switches, replacement MFDs, modeled console, EOTS and inspection zoom are removed. Native instruments remain visible.
- Fixed-wing aircraft share the fitted FS-20 virtual handles and grip poses. Helicopters retain their own controls; original stationary handles are hidden while their VR replacements are active.
- Stick and throttle each have Hold / Toggle grip settings. A monochrome throttle bar and percent/state readout sit on the inboard side of the travel rail.
- Controller diagrams support selecting a physical button and editing its assignment from native Settings or View, with conflict handling and undo.
- Three layout slots per aircraft store the seat, handles and HUD widgets. Slot 1 retains the previous layout. Both-corner widget grabs support moving, tilting and resizing together, with roll locked.
- Look-direction cue size, shape, opacity and visibility are configurable; existing defaults are retained.
- BDF uses green gloves and pale blue-grey sleeves; PALA uses red gloves and black sleeves.
- Stock mission selection/customization dialogs regain their native canvas when open.
- The MIL detent stops just below the native afterburner boundary, avoiding the AFTERBURNER 0% state. Entering either handle’s grab range gives a brief haptic pulse, with hysteresis to prevent repeated pulses from tracking tremor.
