# 0.4.8.17

- Optional **Head clearance** in **VR menu → Seat & handles** clears a small, rounded area of the seat around your head. Off by default; starts at 12 cm, adjustable from 6–20 cm.
- Uses separate local-pilot seat meshes automatically, including supported modded aircraft. Combined or unrecognized seat/cockpit meshes remain unchanged; the menu reports when a seat is unsupported.
- Keeps native textures, collision and ejection behavior. External views retain the original seat. Switching views, losing tracking or disabling the option restores the original geometry.

Includes the mission-preview and scrollable-briefing fix from 0.4.8.16.
